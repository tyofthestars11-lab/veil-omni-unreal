WHAT IS NOT IN THE LIBRARY (Fab is for exactly these)
  - Crystal meshes. 0 crystal meshes outside Paragon's projectile FX; the blue crystal
    mountains in the vision need an asset, not a texture.
  - A water SYSTEM. Lake and ocean MATERIALS exist, so still water can be placed today, but
    there is no flow, no spline river, no waterfall, no Niagara water. Counted: 0 Waterfall,
    0 River anywhere in Content.
  - Vegetation meshes. Landscape grass types only. Counted: 0 Tree, 0 Bush meshes.
  - Market dressing. Counted: 0 Tent, 0 Stall, 0 Awning, 0 barrel/crate/banner. The
    blue-and-gold market tents in the vision have to be bought or modelled.
  - The phi wing. Counted: 0 Wing, 0 Feather, 0 Cloak, and no flight rig. That hero does not
    exist in this library.
  - Bridges. Counted: 0 Bridge meshes in Sicka or Iceland.

HONEST SUMMARY
  The library is strong exactly where the vision is architectural: 33 modular stone pieces,
  33 ancient props, cliffs, roofs, shrines, nine mountains, interior machinery, two complete
  character families with locomotion graphs, one built MetaHuman, water and lava materials,
  fire and smoke FX, 18 sound cues and 38 voice files.
  It is empty exactly where the vision is natural or heroic: crystal geometry, moving water,
  trees, market tents, wings and bridges. Six items, and Fab is the right answer for every
  one of them.

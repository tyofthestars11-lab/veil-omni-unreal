WHAT IS NOT IN THE LIBRARY (Fab is for exactly these)
  - Crystal meshes. The vision's blue crystal mountains need a crystal asset; this project
    owns the TEXTURES for crystal, not the geometry.
  - Water. There is no water system, no river, no waterfall, no flow material. SM_SubAkvo is
    a single submerged mesh, not a body of water.
  - Vegetation. No tree, bush or grass mesh. Iceland ships landscape grass TYPES (LG_A..E),
    which drive a landscape material, not placeable trees.
  - Market dressing. No tent, stall, awning, barrel, crate or banner mesh. The vision's blue
    and gold market tents are not in any pack here.
  - The phi wing. No wing mesh, no cloak mesh, no flight rig. That character does not exist
    in this library.
  - Bridges. No bridge mesh in Sicka or Iceland.

HONEST SUMMARY
  The library is strong exactly where the vision is architectural: warm stone modular walls,
  ancient columns and arches, cliffs, roofs, shrines, mountains, interior machinery, plus two
  complete character families with locomotion graphs and one built MetaHuman.
  It is empty exactly where the vision is natural or heroic: crystals, water, vegetation,
  market props, wings, bridges. Those six are Fab purchases or authored meshes, not code.

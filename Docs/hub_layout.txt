--------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE PLAN IN ONE LINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
One central market plaza at 0,0 with three gold phi pillars standing in it, five streets
leaving it on golden-angle headings, stone buildings in rows down both sides of every
street, tents and stalls inside the plaza, and the Nest cliffs and waterfalls to the west
where the vision puts them. Nothing is placed by dice: every entry below is a named X,Y.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PLAZA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Centre:            0, 0        (the runner's spawn stands here, RUNNER 0,0,100)
Radius:            1100        walkable dressed ground
Floor:             kit stone tiles on a 320 module grid, top face at z = 4
Three gold phi pillars (the vision's market columns), 1200 tall, on an equilateral ring:
  P1   -700,   900
  P2    900,   700
  P3   -100, -1100
  Each: kit column mesh, gold material, phi glyph band at 700 and 1000, collision ON.
Market tents:      6, blue and gold, on a 400 ring at the plaza edge, headings 0, 60, 120,
                   180, 240, 300 deg, each 300 x 300, 240 tall.
Stalls:            12 small stone counters between the tents, 180 x 90, top at z = 110.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------

--------------------------------------------------------------------------

  S1 heading   0.0 deg   runs to  +Y   (north, toward the Spire sightline)
  S2 heading 137.5 deg   runs to  -X,+Y

4. NEST SIDE (west) - cliffs and waterfalls
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Nest rim begins at -6200. Waterfall sheets on the first three shelves, headings
137.5, 210, 282.5 deg, each 900 wide, from shelf top to the ground below.
Wooden bridges: two, from the S5 street end (-5400, 200) across the first fall to the
second shelf. Cliffs are the kit rock mesh, warm stone, no blue tint.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. WHAT IS NOT IN THIS PLAN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Spire, Omni District, Megastructure Deep and the blue crystal range are separate regions of
the same world in the vision. They are not placed in this file, because their coordinates
have to be set once the Hub and Nest ground are one continuous surface. This file is the
Hub and Nest plan only, so the part being built is unambiguous.
